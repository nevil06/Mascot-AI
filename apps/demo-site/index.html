01 / The Challenge

Industrial Decarbonization is Stalled

Cement, steel, and chemical plants generate 30%+ of global emissions. Traditional carbon capture requires high heat, complex solvents, and geological storage that doesn't exist near manufacturing centers.

⚡

Excessive Energy Penalty

Amine scrubbing and Direct Air Capture demand over 2,000 kWh per ton of CO2 captured, wiping out plant profit margins.

🚚

Pipeline Bottlenecks

Most industrial sites are thousands of miles away from certified subsurface sequestration reservoirs.

📉

Negative Unit Economics

Current capture costs exceed $180/ton without yielding any usable secondary byproduct to offset CAPEX.

02 / The Solution

BioGranule™ Modular Mineralization

Our bioengineered carbonic anhydrase catalyst operates at ambient temperatures, instantly crystallizing gaseous CO2 into stable calcium carbonate micro-granules.

🧬

Engineered Biocatalyst

10x faster hydration kinetics than standard enzymes, stable up to 85°C directly in raw flue exhaust gas.

🏭

Plug-and-Play Skid

Modular 40-foot containerized reactors integrate into existing exhaust ductwork in less than 72 hours.

🧱

Sellable Byproduct

Outputs ASTM C33 certified mineral aggregate suitable for ready-mix concrete and sustainable pavers.
